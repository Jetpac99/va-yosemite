Need more travel tips or holiday help?

When is the best time to hike in Yosemite?

Late spring to early autumn is usually best. Waterfalls are often at their boldest in spring, while high-country routes can open later depending on snow.

Do I need a permit?

Some routes and overnight hikes need permits. Always check the latest National Park Service guidance before you travel.

Can beginners enjoy Yosemite trails?

Yes. Mix short valley walks with scenic viewpoints, then build up to longer routes if your boots are feeling persuasive.
